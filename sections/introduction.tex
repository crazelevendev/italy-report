\section{Introduction}
Italy is one of the EU's biggest industrial powers, and France is its third biggest customer \parencite{france-third-client}. Let's try to see what makes Italy so relevant nowadays.
This report originates from a school assignment after not participating in a study trip to Italy, in early November 2026. It exists mainly to discover the ins and outs of Italy.
In this report I will try to answer the following question: \enquote{What are the strengths and weaknesses of Italy in its economic relationship with France?}
The report is structured as follows: first, a presentation of Italy as a country, the company culture in Italy, how its international trade is doing, the economic landscape of Italy, the infrastructure, the environment, the cultural differences with France, a conclusion and a personal assessment.
