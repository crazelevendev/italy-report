\section{Italy as a country}
Italy is a country located in Southern Europe, with a population of about 59 million residents as of January 1st 2026. There were 355,000 births in 2025 (-3.9\% compared to 2024). On average, residents are 47.1 years old and about 25\% of the population is 65 years old or more. About 9\% of the population are foreigners and about 109,000 Italians left the country in 2025. The country's population is only stable thanks to immigration. There are inequalities between the north and the south of Italy and the GDP reflects this, \euro{46,100} per resident in the north-west, vs. \euro{25,000} per resident in the south. A large share of foreign redisents (58\%) choose to live in the north of the country because of that \parencite{istat-demo-ind, istat-gpd}. Companies report that (47\%) of planned hires were hard to fill, mainly due to a lack of candidates (30.2\%) \parencite{camcom-diff-hiring}.
