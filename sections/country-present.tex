\section{Italy as a country}
Italy is a country located in Southern Europe, with a population of about 59 million residents as of January 1st 2026. With 355 000 newborns in 2025 (-3.9\% compared to 2024). On average, residents are 47.1 years old and about 25\% of the population is 65 years old or more. Roughly 10\% of the population are immigrants and about 100 000 Italians are emigrants. The country's popolation is still growing mainly due to immigration, hence the mean age is going to go up \parencite{istat-demo-ind}. This all creates and enforces inequalities, a big part foreigners live in the north of the country (58\%), and the GDP reflects this, 46 100€ per resident in the north-west, vs. 25 000€ per resident in the south.
